About Me

Hello! I am Jannat Sultana, a Computer Science and Engineering graduate. I obtained my BS in CSE from North South University (NSU), Dhaka, Bangladesh.

During my undergraduate studies, I had the privilege of working as an Undergraduate Teaching Assistant at NSU. Currently, I am working as both a Lab Instructor and Graduate Research Assistant at North South University.

I work on projects funded by the ECE Department of NSU under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Rashedur M. Rahman.

My research focuses on developing efficient machine learning systems and advancing natural language processing capabilities.

My work centers on optimizing supervised learning algorithms and pretrained language models for enhanced computational efficiency, with applications spanning Machine Learning, Natural Language Processing, and Computer Vision.

Beyond research, I enjoy traveling to new places and immersing myself in music. These experiences provide inspiration and fresh perspectives for problem solving.
