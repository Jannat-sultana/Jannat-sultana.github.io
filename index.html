Publications

Early detection of occupational stress: Enhancing workplace safety with machine learning and large language models

M. J. Hasan, Jannat Sultana, S. Ahmed, Sifat Momen

PLOS One, 2025

Role: Researcher

paperpdfcodedemo

Abstract

Occupational stress undermines both employee wellbeing and workplace safety, yet most detection methods rely on slow, subjective survey analysis. This work builds a machine-learning pipeline — combining a hybrid RFECV/ANOVA feature-selection step, a 1D-CNN, and several BERT-family language models — on a Malaysian workplace survey dataset. A three-model ensemble reached 90.32% accuracy, the highest reported for this task, and stayed robust across cross-validation and synthetic-data testing. Explainable-AI analysis (SHAP/LIME) traced most of the stress signal to workload, ambiguity, and communication problems, and the resulting model was deployed as a public, real-time screening tool.

Context-Aware Data Cleaning: Optimizing Bengali Text for Contextual Text Classification

M. R. Faisal, A. R. Fahad, S. Z. Ridoy, Jannat Sultana, Z. F. Ria, Md H. Rahman, M. A. Uddin, Rashedur M. Rahman

SN Computer Science, 2025

Role: Researcher

paperpdfcode

Abstract

Bengali NLP pipelines have generally reused text-cleaning recipes built for English, which strip out context that Bengali transformer models actually rely on. This study proposes a context-aware cleaning pipeline — correcting spelling, tagging HTML/URLs, and preserving punctuation and emojis instead of discarding them — and compares it against conventional cleaning across four Bengali benchmark datasets (BEmoC, SentNoB, UBMEC, EmoNoBa). The context-aware approach consistently improved transformer performance, raising accuracy by up to 4% on three of the four datasets, underscoring how much sentence-level context matters for Bengali text classification.

An Efficient Text Cleaning Pipeline for Clinical Text for Transformer Encoder Models

S. Z. Ridoy, Jannat Sultana, Z. F. Ria, M. A. Uddin, Md H. Rahman, Rashedur M. Rahman

2024 IEEE 12th International Conference on Intelligent Systems (IS)

Role: Project Lead

paperpdf

Abstract

With so many text-preprocessing options available for transformer models, it isn't obvious which steps actually help — especially for clinical text, where accuracy stakes are high. This paper tests four common preprocessing techniques and their combinations on MIMIC-III and PubMed data across BERT-base, BioBERT, BioClinicalBERT, and RoBERTa, then proposes a new pipeline that removes repeated punctuation, normalizes text, and uses TF-IDF to filter unimportant words while preserving clinical terms. The proposed pipeline outperformed the baselines (90.16% accuracy on MIMIC-III, 64.20% on PubMed with BioBERT), while cutting training time by up to 17%.
